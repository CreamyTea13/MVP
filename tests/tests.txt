# Tests

## Test 1 - Compare prices and handle ties [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Covers: homepage navigation, calculator output, equal-price guardrail, related tool routing
Description: Verify the main calculation workflow and the corrected tie behavior.
Steps:
1. Open Unit Price Compare from the homepage.
2. Enter Option A price 12, quantity 24; Option B price 8, quantity 10. Calculate.
3. Confirm Option A costs $0.50 per unit and Option B costs $0.80 per unit.
4. Change Option B price to 5 and quantity to 10. Calculate.
Expected: The first comparison recommends Option A; the second says Same unit price and does not recommend one as cheaper.

## Test 2 - Search and calculate overtime
Viewport: desktop
Covers: search, filtering, overtime calculation, sharing control
Description: Search selects the relevant tool and produces correct pay.
Steps:
1. Search for overtime on the homepage, then open Overtime Pay Calculator.
2. Enter rate 20 and hours 45. Calculate.
Expected: Estimated gross pay is $950.00, regular pay $800.00, overtime $150.00, and Share result is available.

## Test 3 - Mobile category, painting and invalid quantity
Viewport: mobile
Covers: mobile layout, category filtering, paint calculation, invalid quantity guardrail
Description: Verify useful mobile workflows without horizontal overflow.
Steps:
1. Filter the homepage to Home, open Paint Calculator, enter area 800, coats 2, coverage 400 and calculate.
2. Return home and open Unit Price Compare. Enter prices 12 and 8, quantities 0 and 10, then calculate.
Expected: Painting recommends 4 gallons. Zero quantity produces Check your inputs. Forms and results remain usable at 375px width.

## Test 4 - Block anonymous and forged download access
Viewport: mobile
Covers: fulfillment form, POST /api/fulfillment/redeem, anonymous denial, forged reference rejection, mobile error recovery
Description: Download access is never granted from merely visiting a URL.
Steps:
1. Open /fulfillment/ with no query parameters. Confirm there is no Download Everyday Engine Plus button visible.
2. Enter not-a-purchase as the purchase reference and choose Verify purchase.
3. Enter cs_test_abcdefghijklmnopqrstuv and choose Verify purchase.
Expected: Invalid or unverified references show a useful error, and no archive becomes available. With no configured secrets, secure delivery is unavailable. No Purchase complete message appears.

Covers: Plus page, GET /api/commerce/status, paused-checkout guardrail, backend failure presentation
Description: Customers cannot start a payment before verification, including when commerce status fails.


2. Confirm the $9 one-time product description is shown.
Expected: Purchases are temporarily unavailable, and no active purchase link is shown.
